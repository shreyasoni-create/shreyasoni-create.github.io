I’m a Shopify Developer who enjoys turning visual ideas into polished, responsive storefronts. My work often starts with a Figma design: understanding the layout, identifying reusable elements, and deciding where Shopify's native sections can be configured and where a custom section is needed.

I build and customize Shopify themes using Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This includes custom sections with merchant-editable settings, product and collection layouts, variant presentation, responsive behavior, and storefront UI improvements.

I have also worked with metafields, metaobjects, payment and shipping integrations, and third-party apps. Alongside frontend development, I’m building practical backend knowledge through Node.js, Express, REST APIs, GraphQL, webhooks, and Git/GitHub.

I value clear communication, reliable delivery, attention to detail, and creating long-term working relationships through thoughtful development and continuous improvement.

02 / THEME DEVELOPMENT

Custom Sections

Build reusable Online Store 2.0 sections with schema settings that let merchants update headings, images, content, and other storefront elements.

03 / STOREFRONT

Theme Customization

Customize product pages, collection layouts, product variants, navigation, and storefront components to suit a brand's design.
