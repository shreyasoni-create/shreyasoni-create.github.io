My approach

From design reference to working storefront.

A practical workflow focused on responsive design, maintainable components, and real Shopify functionality.

01 / PLAN

Understand the design

Review Figma references, identify reusable patterns, and decide where custom Shopify sections are needed.

02 / BUILD

Develop & configure

Build with Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with appropriate section settings and merchant controls.

03 / TEST

Refine the experience

Check responsive layouts, troubleshoot using browser DevTools, and refine the customer experience.
