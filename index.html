02 / About Me

Design-minded developer. Shopify-focused.

“I enjoy turning a visual reference into a real storefront that looks considered, works responsively, and remains easy to manage.”

My work centres on building and customizing Shopify storefronts, with a particular strength in translating Figma designs into responsive, reusable theme sections.

I review designs, identify reusable components, decide when a standard Shopify section is sufficient, and build custom Liquid sections when the design calls for more control. I pay close attention to spacing, typography, responsive layouts, and how a store feels across desktop and mobile.

Alongside storefront development, I work with Shopify apps and e-commerce integrations. I'm also developing my backend skills with Node.js, APIs, webhooks, and integration workflows connected to real Shopify use cases.
