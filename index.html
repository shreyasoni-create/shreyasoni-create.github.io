A Little About Me

Turning design ideas into functional storefronts.

I'm a Shopify developer with a frontend-focused approach to ecommerce development. My strongest area is translating Figma designs into responsive Shopify sections and customizing storefronts to meet specific design needs.

I work with Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build reusable theme components, customize product and collection pages, and create merchant-editable settings through Shopify section schemas.

I'm also growing my backend capabilities with Node.js, Express, REST APIs, and webhooks, with a focus on practical Shopify integrations.
